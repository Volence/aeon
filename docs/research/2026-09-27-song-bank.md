# S2CLIP-MTZ-SONG-BANK: research and pricing (2026-09-27)

Research only. No engine or data change is on this branch. It answers the booking in
`docs/DEFERRED_WORK.md` (`## S2CLIP-MTZ-SONG-BANK`): how the driver banks today, how the
references do it, what each lever costs, and which to take.

Labels: **MEASURED** means run here. **READ** means read in source and not executed.
**INFERRED** means arithmetic or reasoning from those, not run.

## 1. How the driver banks today

**Room in the one sound bank (MEASURED).** The measurements come from a canonical plain
build of `origin/master` 3bf61778 in this worktree (`s4.bin` CRC 8319c1b4, which matches
the master note) and from `emit_sound_blob` artifact sizes:

| | plain | DEBUG |
|---|---|---|
| bank ($17, LMA 0xB8000, window $8000) | 32,768 | 32,768 |
| engine-table head (`soundbankhead.emp`: sound tables 985, default pitch 264, SfxBlobWinTab 274, SeqOpcodeTable 64, DacSampleTable 120, pad 5) | 1,712 | 1,712 |
| songs + SongTable/SongPatchTable (`mt_bank.emp`) | 20,176 | 26,920 |
| SFX block (`sfx_bank`) | 1,604 | 1,603 |
| **free** | **9,276** | **2,533** |

The plain figures come from the listing (`Song_MovingTrucks` 0xB86B0, `Sfx_33` 0xBD580,
SFX end 0xBDBC4). The DEBUG figures come from the emitted `mt_bank_body_debug.bin` plus its
tables. The DEBUG figure agrees with the booking's overflow: 2,533 B free minus MTZ's
~3,072 B is 539 B short, and the booking reported 540 B over.

A song costs its converted size, plus 8 B of table entries, plus up to 1 B of pad. Sizes
MEASURED with the shipped converter: EHZ 2,528+288, CPZ 3,613+192, MTZ 2,875+192. HPZ, WFZ
and OOZ are refused on master (HPZ by the packer, WFZ by `dMidTimpani`/`dVLowTimpani`, OOZ by
`fTone_0C`).

**Per-song banking already exists on the parameter path (READ):**

- 68k side. `Sound_PlayMusic` derives the bank from each song's own ROM address:
  `z80_bank(d1, a1)` at `engine/sound/sound_api.emp:295`. It posts `PARAM_BANK`, the song
  window pointer and the patch window pointer. Nothing on the 68k side assumes a single bank.
- Z80 side. `Snd_LoadSong` stores `SND_MUSIC_PARAM_BANK` into `SND_SONG_BANK`
  (`engine/sound/z80_sound_driver.emp:1542-1543`) and banks it in (`:1575-1576`). Each
  frame, `Run_SeqFrame_OnSongBank` (`:1252-1257`) switches to `SND_SONG_BANK`, runs
  `Sequencer_Frame`, then switches back to the sample bank.
- `SndDrv_SetBank` (`:1221`) takes any bank id below $100. It writes only the $6000 latch,
  so it is DMA-safe, and it is cached.
- DAC samples already live in other banks (`dac_banks`, $15/$16). `Snd_StartSample` stashes
  the descriptor's bank (`:1144-1145`), and the frame brackets switch song bank to sample
  bank every tick while a drum plays. Switching banks every frame is already routine.

**Where the single-bank assumption lives (READ).** It is in the data the Z80 reads through
the window while a song bank is in:

- **The engine-table head is baked at fixed window addresses** ($8000..$86B0). The resident
  Z80 code reads these tables at their VMAs:
  - `SeqOpcodeTable` (the `.coord` dispatch, `engine/sound/sound_sequencer.emp:1172`);
  - `DacSampleTable` (`$E2` from music, `sound_sequencer.emp:2054-2057`);
  - `SndDefaultPitchTable` (`engine/sound/sound_fm.emp:881-884`);
  - the FM/PSG LUTs and envelope tables.

  These reads are correct only when the bank in the window has the head at offset 0.
- `mt_bank.emp:284-301` holds nine `ensure(bankid(X) == bankid("MovingTrucks_Bank_Start"))`,
  one per song and patch bank.
- **The SFX path re-uploads music voices under the SFX bank.** Three places:
  - `Sfx_Frame` banks `SFX_BLOB_BANK` in first (`engine/sound/sound_sfx.emp:263-264`). Today
    that is a cached no-op because the song and SFX banks are the same bank.
  - `Sfx_Restore .fm` (`sound_sfx.emp:1293-1301`) calls `Fm_PatchPtr`/`Fm_PatchLoad` on the
    MUSIC channel's patch through `SND_SEQ_PATCHTAB` with the SFX bank still in the window.
  - `Sfx_UnpauseRestore` (`:1512-1514`) banks `SFX_BLOB_BANK` and then reads music patches.

  If a song lived in another bank, all three would read the wrong bytes.
- **The per-song pitch table.** `Fm_NoteFromTable` uses `Snd_PitchTabPtr` (song base +
  offset) whenever it is nonzero, including under the SFX bank. MEASURED from the headers:
  only Moving Trucks sets one (`$30B1`). EHZ, CPZ, MTZ and HCZ2 are all 0, so they use the
  default table, which is present in every bank that carries the head.
- About 14 comments in `engine/sound` and `games/sonic4/data/sound` state co-location
  ("co-located", "same bank", "== that bank"). They mark reasoning that would need
  re-checking.

**Verdict on item 1 (READ, INFERRED).** Per-song banking is a small generalisation of the
existing mechanism, not a new one:

- The bank parameter, the per-frame song-bank bracket and the cached SetBank already exist.
- What is missing is (i) a copy of the head at offset 0 of every song bank, and (ii) switching
  to the song bank wherever the SFX path touches a music patch.
- **Interrupts do not make this harder (READ).** The idle tick and the Timer-A tick both run
  `Sequencer_Frame` with interrupts off (`z80_sound_driver.emp:385-395`, the sample loop is
  `di` end to end), and `Snd_PollMailbox_Banked` (`:675-687`) restores the bank it found. So
  the VBlank ISR cannot land mid-frame on the wrong bank.

## 2. References

- **Sonic 3 & Knuckles** (`skdisasm/Sound/Z80 Sound Driver.asm`, READ):
  - Per-song bank byte table `z80_MusicBanks` (`:2841`), read at load (`:1793-1810`) into
    `zSongBank`. Songs span several `startBank` banks (`sonic3k.asm:201051` onward).
  - SFX live in a separate `SndBank`. Every frame the driver switches to the song bank for
    music (`:705-707`), then to the SFX bank (`bankswitchToSFX`, `:730`).
  - Its pointer tables and universal voice bank sit in Z80 RAM (`!org 1300h`), so no bank
    needs a table head.
- **Flamedriver in S.C.E.** (`Sound/Flamedriver.asm`, READ):
  - Same per-song `z80_MusicBanks` (`:4742`, `:1981-1989`).
  - `Music_Master_Table` (`:4935-4951`) is a macro meant to sit at **the same window offset
    in every music bank**. It `fatal`s on "Inconsistent placement ... on bank". That is the
    head-replica pattern lever (a) needs. `DAC_Master_Table` does the same for DAC banks.
  - When an SFX ends and a music voice is restored, Flamedriver switches to the song bank,
    uploads the instrument, and switches back to the SFX bank (`:3602-3610`). That is the
    `Sfx_Restore` fix lever (a) needs.
- **Sonic 2** (`s2disasm/s2.sounddriver.asm`, READ):
  - Two music banks, `MusicPoint1` and `MusicPoint2`, one bit per song in the master
    playlist (`:3817`, `zBankSwitchToMusic` `:2840-2848`).
  - Most songs are Saxman-compressed and decompressed into Z80 RAM (`zSaxmanDec`, `:1796`),
    so they play from RAM and not through the window. Only uncompressed songs stream from ROM.
  - aeon streams every song from ROM, so the S2 route (decompress to RAM) would need a RAM
    song buffer that aeon's 8 KB Z80 RAM does not have (INFERRED: the debug resident blob is
    6,330 B and driver state starts at $18F0).

**Risk, per lever:**

- **DMA-survival DAC.**
  - (a) and (b): SetBank writes only the latch and is DMA-safe (READ). The Timer-A tick
    already defers on `SND_CTRL_DMA_ACTIVE` before any banked read. An extra uncached switch
    per frame (song bank to SFX bank) costs ~270 T. That figure is INFERRED from the
    `SndDrv_SetBank` instruction list: 8×(7+4+13) plus the fixed part. It is about 0.45% of a
    59,659-T frame, and it lands inside a DAC-active tick, so the ring-lead budget must be
    re-measured. The FILL/DRAIN cycle-balance `ensure`s do not cover the tick path (READ), so
    they will not catch it.
  - (c): none.
- **SFX during a bank switch.**
  - (a) and (b): the three co-location sites in §1 are the real hazard. A missed one plays a
    wrong voice when an SFX ends over a bank-2 song. Only a gate that plays an FM SFX over a
    bank-2 song and checks the restored voice would catch it. The ~14 co-location comments are
    the audit list.
  - (c): none.
- **Gates and goldens that move** (READ; none run):
  - Any lever changes ROM bytes. That means sigil's whole-ROM goldens for the sound-on shapes,
    plus `mt_bank_port.rs`, `mt_dual_carrier.rs` and `seam2_layout_derivation.rs`. Byte-moving
    is routine, and aeon lands alone.
  - aeon: `tools/test_smps_import.py` (MTZ joins the generator), `tools/test_sfx_bank_wiring.py`,
    `tools/test_sound_head_envelopes.py`, the `MT_PITCHTAB_OFFSET` ensure, and the nine bankid
    ensures, which (a) rewrites.
  - (a) and (b) also change the resident Z80 blob. INFERRED ~54 B of slack below $18F0 in
    DEBUG (the blob is 6,330 B), so 10-12 B fits.
  - (a) and (b) may trip `tools/bganim_room.py` / `clip_anchors.py` if the new bank moves
    anchors.
  - c1 removes the subject of `tools/hcz2_s3k_balance.py`, which pokes `SONG_HCZ2` into
    `s4.debug.bin`. HCZ2 and DrumTest are played by game code only in the off-canonical
    hotkeys shape (`games/sonic4/debug/game_debug.emp`, whole-file gated on
    `SOUND_DEBUG_HOTKEYS`). c1 must keep them there, which means gating them on the hotkeys
    flag and not on `DEBUG`. UNVERIFIED: which `-D` flags the `config_a` profile passes.

## 4. Recommendation

**Take (a), the per-song bank with a head replica, as the fix.**

- **Reason:** it is the only lever that scales to the mega-act's "every zone brings its song".
  Six S2 songs plus our own will not fit one 32 KB window, and both S3K and Flamedriver solved
  it this way.
- In aeon it is a small generalisation. The bank parameter, the per-frame song-bank bracket and
  the cached SetBank already exist. The Z80 change is two patch-bank sites plus one pitch-table
  guard.
- (b) spends the same machinery to free 1.6 KB. It is dominated.

**If Metropolis must play before (a) lands,** c1 (take HCZ2 + DrumTest out of the canonical
DEBUG shape and keep them in the hotkeys shape) unblocks MTZ, WFZ and OOZ in both canonical
shapes as a data-only aeon change. It costs `hcz2_s3k_balance.py` its canonical subject. It is
a stopgap, not the end state. Once (a) exists, HCZ2 moves to bank 2 and comes back.

**Order inside (a):**

1. Ask the sigil lane what a new 0x8000-aligned song-bank section needs: a `section_align` row,
   and whether it also needs an anchor or frozen row.
2. Land the Z80 patch-bank brackets. They are correct and inert while every song shares the SFX
   bank.
3. Add the bank-2 section.
4. Gate it with an FM SFX that ends over a bank-2 song, reading the restored voice.

## Sigil's answer on the build side (sigil-66, 2026-09-28, a SOURCE READING at sigil master 2209c525 and aeon a2400d79; nothing built or probed)

Recorded here so it survives a session boundary; re-derive before acting on any of it.

1. **Required:** a `DECLARED` row in `sigil-harness/src/section_align.rs` keyed by the new section's head label, e.g. `d("<head label>", 0x8000, Z80_BANK_WINDOW)`. An undeclared section is refused by name (`native.rs` `validate_declared_alignment`, ~2501). `map.toml` cannot declare alignment today (that is SIGIL-DECOUPLE step 2, aeon's to sequence). Size S, standing-rule material.
2. **Island or not, which depends on our design:**
   - (a) LMA-only section (vma == lma; songs reached through SetBank with window offsets computed in the data): the packing walk rounds it up (`packed_chained_base`); no anchor, no frozen row. Caveat: it would be the FIRST walk-rounded 0x8000 section (existing ones are anchored so the walk never rounds them); up to 32 KB padding lands wherever the order puts it and shifts everything after.
   - (b) Phase bank (vma = 0x8000 != lma, like `soundbankhead`): an island held at a FROZEN row; needs a sigil frozen-table row plus an aeon `[[anchor]]` (vma, `when = "sound_on"`) landed TOGETHER. The one aeon change that still cannot land alone. S to M, paired. "If your head embeds carry Z80 labels resolved at window addresses, you are in (b)."
3. **Possibly the largest part:** `validate_sound_fold` (`native.rs` ~3310, always on). seam-2 (`emit_sound_blob` / `seam2::sound_layout`) PREDICTS the bases of exactly two blobs (`Song_MovingTrucks`, `Sfx_33`), bakes absolute pointers against the prediction, then checks prediction == placement. If bank-2 songs go through `emit_sound_blob` with baked pointers, `sound_layout` must predict a third base: M, silent-garbled-audio class. If they are `.emp` embeds whose pointers the linker resolves, no seam-2 change. A new bank placed BEFORE MT/SFX in the order shifts their predicted bases (caught loudly by the fold gate; `sound_layout` must model its length).
4. Goldens: aeon lands on its own gates; sigil refreezes and resyncs `repin_pins.rs` after (S). `mt_bank_port` / `mt_dual_carrier` / `seam2_layout_derivation` break only if MT/SFX bases or their derivation move.

Rough total: S for (a) with no seam-2 involvement; S-M paired for (b); +M if seam-2 must predict the bank. Sigil offers a probe (build a copy of our tree with a stub second bank, report which gates fire) once we pick a shape; not started.
