# Falling like Sonic 3: no cap, a swept move, S3K's airborne camera (2026-09-27)

Owner ruling **FALL-FEEL** (`docs/decisions.jsonl`, chose D): *"I'd prefer it be like sonic 3 but
if we can try to capture that feeling without needing too much that'd be great. Unfortunately C
drifts too far from sonic games."* It supersedes the cap half of FALL-CAP-15 (`d-18-answered`);
its safety half, that Sonic never passes through a floor, stands.

Branch `feel/s3k-fall`, based on `origin/master` `6f30b5ce`. Tools and raw results:
`docs/research/2026-09-27-s3k-fall/`. Every measurement is the headless `oracle-aether`
subprocess (md5 `3e7c2778`), sigil `5a68be69` / emit `776e087f` for every build in this
document (the pair was already the swapped one when the first build ran; base DEBUG crc
`011cdbc8` is on that pair; no build straddled the swap). No MCP.

Straight fall from rest; `screen` is what shipped (uncapped, 24 px airborne camera, our focal
line, the residency and screen holds; y_vel left alone while held):

| fall | before (cap 15, cam 16) | S3K exactly | shipped | lowest sprite line, shipped |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1,024 px | 104 frames, lands 15.0 px/f | 98, 21.4 | 98, 21.4 | 144 |
| 2,048 px | 172, 15.0 | 138, 30.2 | 140, 30.6 | 181 (held 12 frames) |
| 5,400 px (OJZ) | 395, 15.0 | 223, 48.8 (off screen 81 frames) | 279, 61.0 | 181 (held 151 frames, never off screen) |

Under ~1,300 px it is S3K's fall exactly. Past that S3K's own 24 px camera loses Sonic off the
bottom of the screen; here he rides the bottom edge instead. In both the world never scrolls
faster than the camera's 24 px/frame, so the scroll the eye sees is S3K's; what differs is that
Sonic stays visible and lands later (279 frames against S3K's 223 on 5,400 px, with a faster
landing speed because y_vel keeps growing while he is held).

## What was built

1. **No cap** at the three sites above; gravity adds saturate at $7FFF instead of wrapping.
2. **The swept move** (`Air_Move` / `Player_AirMoveSwept`, `games/sonic4/player/player_air.emp`).
   At or under `PHYS_SWEEP_STEP` ($F00: one pixel under `COLL_CELL_H`, whole pixels) one
   `ObjectMove`, the whole cost one compare. Faster, 2^k equal parts (k the fewest halvings that
   bring `|y_vel|` under the step; each part an exact 16.16 delta, so a frame that meets nothing
   ends on ObjectMove's subpixel) with a probe after every part but the last:
   * falling, `Air_SweepFloor`: the landing pair, stopping only inside `Air_FloorLandBanded`'s
     eligibility, so a stop is always a landing; a deeper hit is a wall beside the fall and must
     not stall it;
   * GLIDE and GLIDEFALL, `Glide_FloorProbe`: the glide family's centre probe, the one
     `Glide_Collide` lands on;
   * rising, `Air_SweepCeiling`: any embedded ceiling (every class resolves it).
   **Three-cell probe, rejected:** the probe reads forward of the feet, and a tunnelling frame is
   one whose feet ended past the floor, so extra forward reach sees nothing. It helps only as
   predictive landing, which is this sweep with a fixed reach and still needs a rule above
   32/48 px. Cost: ~1.5k cycles per intermediate probe; 4-6k a tick at 40-60 px/frame
   (`results/profile_diag_fall.txt`), nothing below 15 px/frame. Steep-slope landing: the snap
   and banding run unchanged in the state's own collide step, at an embed of at most one part.
3. **The camera**: `CAM_AIR_Y_STEP` 24 while the leader is airborne, 16 grounded. Streaming
   bounds follow `CAM_MAX_Y_STEP` 24: `VFILL_ROWS_PER_FRAME` 2 -> 4 (the up-fill moves in row
   pairs, so 3 buys nothing upward), `FG_PEAK_BYTES` 3 rows, hence `BG_OVERWRITE_CHUNK_BYTES`
   1824 -> 1696 (the test's own derivation) and `BG_WIPE_DMA_ROWS` 14 -> 13.
4. **Axis B — residency, answered by holding the player, not by filling faster.**
   `Collision_ResidentBottom` (engine) returns the first row the ring has WRITTEN (the pending
   partial row and the odd-row collision copy both counted). `Player_FallLimit` keeps the floor
   probe's lowest read (feet + one cell) above it, and holds the camera leader's sprite on the
   bottom edge of the screen the camera showed last frame (not when the camera is parked at
   `Camera_Y_Max`, so pits stay fallable). The position waits, y_vel does not.
5. Derived: `LL_STEP_MAX_Y` 16 -> 128 (the y_vel word's reach; a fast fall is a step, not a
   teleport, to the layer lines; `tools/layer_line_gate.py` re-aimed to read both bounds);
   `PBOUND_BOTTOM_MARGIN`'s fall-cap ensure deleted with its reason; `REGION_MIN_SPAN` kept at
   32 with an ensure that it exceeds the step; `BLOCK_SPEC_LEAD_TICKS` kept on the ground step.

**SUB-CELL FLOORS — a pre-existing hole this parcel does not close, and neither did the cap.**

## What it cost against "without needing too much"

* **Correctness was cheap.** No cap, the sweep, the holds and the residency query are ~300
  lines of code, cost nothing below 15 px/frame, and keep every floor solid and every probe
  inside the ring (above).
* **The Sonic 3 camera is not cheap here.** At 24 px/frame the vertical streaming does 50% more
  row work, and OJZ act 1 makes every exit from section 0 a 5,300 px fall, so the lag the owner
  already feels in those falls grows: 62 -> 345 lag frames over the DEBUG leg set, 6 -> 33 in
  release; on the clip's pit, 12 -> 135.

**Options (the owner's call):**
1. **Keep it as built** (S3K's camera, this branch's tip). The fall reads as Sonic 3's; long
   diagonal falls drop about one frame in 5-20 (DEBUG) or 1 in 100 (release).
2. **Air camera back to 16**, everything else kept (one constant pair, `CAM_AIR_Y_STEP` and
   `CAM_MAX_Y_ROWS`): falls still accelerate uncapped, but past ~16 px/frame Sonic sinks to the
   bottom edge and the world scrolls at 16, which is the old look with a faster landing. Lag 176
   / 23 as measured.
3. **Make the 24 affordable**: the fill's per-row cost (RPC-1, booked: ~22 -> 15 and ~38 -> 20
   cycles per word in the copy loops) and a streaming-aware cap that slows the airborne camera
   only while the fill is behind. Not built, not measured.
4. **Content**: floors in OJZ sections 1-8 remove the 5,300 px falls that make this matter
   (ojz-feel option E).

## Method notes

* Red-first, all on disk, restored from the committed baseline (`results/redfirst_*.txt`):
  `PHYS_SWEEP_STEP` raised to a whole cell, given a subpixel, and re-typed as a literal under a
  lowered `COLL_CELL_H` — each RED on its ensure; `COLLISION_CELL_SHIFT` lowered — GREEN under
  the old spelling (FINDING: the old tautological ensure followed it while the probes still read
  16 px cells), RED now that the shift is pinned to `COLL_CELL_H`; `VFILL_ROWS_PER_FRAME` 3,
  `CAM_MAX_Y_ROWS` 2, `CAM_AIR_Y_STEP` below the ground step, `SPRITE_CULL_MARGIN` 16,
  `REGION_MIN_SPAN` 24, `LL_STEP_MAX_Y` 8 — each RED. The witness: sweep disabled -> 795
  pass-throughs, exit 1. The layer-line gate: RED on the canonical clip build before its re-aim
  (4 disagreements, `results/layer_line_gate_red_before_reaim.txt`), RED on a routine bounded
  at the X step (37), GREEN after.
* FINDING (sigil, not fixed): `lea -SW_FRAME(sp), sp` with a const displacement was DROPPED by
  the contract closure; spelled `suba.w`/`adda.w`.
* Not run: the replay net (`replay_runner` is not built in this checkout). Neither fixture
  reaches |y_vel| > $F00 (DEFERRED_WORK, REPLAY-TERMINAL-FALL-COVERAGE), so the sweep and the
  holds cannot move it; the 24 px camera and the fill cap of 4 could, only where the camera owes
  more than 16 px in one tick.
* New cross-module names (sigil's `*_port` rigs may need them): `Air_Move`, `Air_MoveAs`,
  `Glide_FloorProbe`, `Collision_ResidentBottom`, `CAM_GROUND_Y_STEP`, `CAM_AIR_Y_STEP`,
  `CAM_MAX_Y_ROWS`, `PHYS_SWEEP_STEP`, `PHYS_SWEEP_SHIFT_MAX`, `LL_STEP_MAX_X/Y` (now engine
  constants), `ST_IN_AIR` in camera.emp; `PHYS_FALL_CAP` is gone.
